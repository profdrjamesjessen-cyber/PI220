PI220 REVISION 011 — OFFICIAL SOUNDTRACK INTEGRATION

Included:
- Official original theme: Beyond the Horizon — Extended Edition v1.0
- Existing taskbar sound control opens a compact sound panel.
- User-initiated Play/Pause and Stop controls.
- Independent volume slider.
- Sound is stopped and muted by default; no autoplay.
- Volume preference is stored locally where browser storage is available.
- Existing window manager, applications, terminal and boot sequence retained.
- Existing PI220Audio public interface retained and extended.
